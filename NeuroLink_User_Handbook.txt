FOREWORD: OUR COMMUNITY PHILOSOPHY

    In modern clinical medicine and cognitive neuroscience, a persistent gap
    often separates bench research from bedside clinical practice. Clinicians
    routinely encounter unique case variations and real-world treatment
    trajectories, while computational neuroscientists and biomedical engineers
    work in parallel on signal processing models, functional imaging, and brain-
    computer interfaces (BCI). Without an open, structured, and credible medium
    for collaboration, valuable medical insights remain scattered across
    isolated notes and informal discussions.

    NeuroLink was designed to solve this challenge. Inspired by the pioneering
    work of health communities like PatientsLikeMe, NeuroLink approaches shared
    medical and scientific discussions as real-world evidence. We believe that
    when licensed physicians and scientific researchers can readily discover each
    other's work, engage in peer consultation, and receive tangible credit for
    high-quality educational contributions, the entire scientific community
    advances.

    This handbook serves as your complete guide to the platform. Whether you are
    a neurologist seeking inter-hospital case discussions, a researcher sharing
    computational neural decoding findings, or a healthcare worker exploring the
    latest literature, this document explains not only how each tool functions,
    but why it was built and how to use it safely and effectively.

CHAPTER 1: INTRODUCTION TO NEUROLINK AND SYSTEM BOUNDARIES
================================================================================

1.1 Mission and Vision

    NeuroLink is a dedicated social knowledge network tailored for healthcare
    and neuroscience professionals. It provides a collaborative environment
    where medical doctors, university researchers, clinical fellows, and
    biomedical engineers can share clinical observations, debate published
    literature, and build multidisciplinary research connections.

    Unlike generic consumer social networks, interactions on NeuroLink are
    structured around professional integrity. Clinical opinions carry context
    through verified professional badges, and meaningful contributions are
    recognized through an objective, peer-evaluated credit system.


1.2 Supported User Groups

    The platform explicitly recognizes four primary participant categories,
    each with tailored roles and capabilities:

    1. General Community Members
       This group includes patients, caregivers, undergraduate biology students,
       and members of the public interested in brain health. General members can
       read public educational posts, participate in constructive discussions,
       and follow verified specialists. They do not undergo professional
       credential verification and do not submit posts for reward credits.

    2. Physicians and Healthcare Professionals
       Licensed medical doctors, clinical neurologists, neuroradiologists, and
       rehabilitation physicians. Members in this tier verify their practicing
       licenses, share clinical perspectives, participate in inter-hospital case
       consultations, and earn credits redeemable for continuing medical
       education (CME) resources.

    3. Researchers and Academic Scientists
       Principal investigators, postdoctoral fellows, neural engineers, and
       graduate research students working on brain-computer interfaces, electro-
       encephalography (EEG) analysis, and cognitive science. They verify their
       institutional affiliations and ORCID records, share summaries of recent
       findings, and redeem credits for scientific conference attendance.

    4. Platform Administrators and Clinical Reviewers
       Board members and appointed clinical reviewers responsible for vetting
       practicing licenses, scoring scientific contributions across five rigorous
       criteria, auditing the credit ledger, and maintaining community safety.


1.3 Important Scope Restrictions and System Boundaries

    To prevent confusion regarding the scope of NeuroLink, users must keep the
    following architectural boundaries in mind:

    - Independence from External EEG Repositories:
      NeuroLink operates as a standalone social knowledge platform. It does not
      access, copy, migrate, or integrate with any external EEG website dataset
      or external patient database.

    - No Automated Medical Diagnostic AI:
      The platform does not perform automated clinical EEG signal diagnosis or
      prescribe patient treatments. All discussions, case summaries, and shared
      imaging studies represent educational knowledge exchanges among peers and
      must never replace the clinical judgment of an attending physician.

CHAPTER 2: GETTING STARTED AND ACCOUNT REGISTRATION
================================================================================

2.1 Creating Your Account

    Joining NeuroLink takes less than two minutes. Open your web browser and go
    to the registration screen at http://localhost:5173/register or click the
    "Tham gia ngay" button on the home page.

    The registration form asks for four basic details:
    1. Display Name: Your full professional name, such as "Dr. Tran Van Minh".
    2. Username: A unique handle (lowercase letters, numbers, and underscores),
       such as "dr_minh", which appears in your profile URL and mentions.
    3. Email Address: An active email address used for account security.
    4. Password: A secure passphrase of at least 8 characters.

    When you submit the form, the backend hashes your password using the bcrypt
    cryptographic algorithm, stores your account securely in PostgreSQL, and
    issues a secure, HttpOnly session cookie to your browser. You are immediately
    logged in and redirected to the main feed.


2.2 Logging In and Session Security

    To sign in on subsequent visits, navigate to http://localhost:5173/login. You
    can enter either your username or your email address along with your
    password.

    NeuroLink employs strict security safeguards to protect your account:
    - Session tokens are stored in an encrypted database table and tied to your
      browser session using HttpOnly cookies with Lax SameSite protection.
      This prevents malicious scripts from stealing your login credentials.
    - Rate limiters actively monitor login attempts from each network address,
      temporarily blocking rapid-fire attempts to guess passwords.
    - Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) tokens are validated on all sensitive
      requests, ensuring commands originate only from your authenticated browser
      session.


2.3 Development and Sandbox Demo Accounts

    If you are exploring the platform in a local test environment, the database
    comes pre-loaded with representative accounts across all key roles:

CHAPTER 3: UNDERSTANDING THE USER INTERFACE AND DAILY NAVIGATION
================================================================================

3.1 The Three-Column Desktop Workspace

    Once logged in, the application presents a balanced, three-column layout
    designed to minimize clutter and keep your focus on professional exchanges:

    - Left Navigation Sidebar:
      Displays your personal avatar and quick profile summary. Below your card,
      you will find dedicated navigation links to the Main Feed, Specialist
      Directory, Private Messages, Notifications, Contributions Center, and
      Credits & Rewards. The bottom of the sidebar lists active clinical
      hashtags such as #LamSangThanKinh and #BCI_Neurotech for one-click topic
      filtering.

    - Central Content Stream:
      Houses the clinical post composer at the top, followed by a feed mode
      toggle ("All Posts" versus "Following"), and an infinite-scrolling timeline
      of scholarly posts.

    - Right Recommendation Rail:
      Surfaces recommended neurologists, fellows, and neurotechnology
      researchers who share your clinical or computational interests, complete
      with one-click "Follow" shortcuts.


3.2 Top Navigation Bar and Mobile Responsiveness

    Across the top of every screen, the global header provides:
    - The NeuroLink brand icon, returning you to the main feed from anywhere.
    - A global search input for finding colleagues, conditions, and papers.
    - Quick-access icons for unread messages and notifications, complete with
      real-time badge counters that refresh automatically.
    - Your user avatar menu, containing direct links to your profile, account
      settings, system administration (if you hold the Admin role), and log out.

    When accessing NeuroLink on tablets or mobile phones, the desktop sidebars
    tuck away cleanly, and a persistent bottom navigation bar appears, giving you
    one-tap access to the feed, search, messages, notifications, and profile.

CHAPTER 4: MANAGING YOUR PERSONAL AND PROFESSIONAL PROFILE
================================================================================

4.1 Public Profile Elements

    Your profile serves as your academic and clinical curriculum vitae on
    NeuroLink. Anyone viewing your profile at http://localhost:5173/u/your_username
    sees your full display name, professional bio, avatar, follower count, and
    a reverse-chronological timeline of your published articles.

    If you are a verified professional, your profile prominently displays a
    specialist badge:
    - Doctors and Clinicians receive an emerald medical badge indicating their
      clinical specialty, hospital affiliation, and years of practice.
    - Researchers and Scientists receive a purple academic badge detailing their
      research institute, laboratory focus, and direct link to their ORCID profile.


4.2 Updating Profile Information

    To update your details, open the user menu in the top right header and select
    "Cai dat tai khoan" (Settings). Under the "Thong tin ca nhan" tab, you can:
    - Change your display name and upload a new profile picture.
    - Revise your biographical statement to reflect recent fellowships or
      ongoing grant projects.
    - Review your contact email address.

    After editing your information, click "Luu thay doi" (Save Changes). The
    changes reflect immediately across the entire platform.


4.3 Changing Your Password

    Also located in the Settings area is the "Bao mat" (Security) panel. To
    update your password, enter your current password once, followed by your new
    password and its confirmation.

    When you confirm the password change, the backend updates your password hash
    and revokes all other active sessions across any other computers or browsers
    you might have logged into previously, safeguarding your account if a device
    was left unattended.

CHAPTER 5: EXPLORING THE COMMUNITY AND FINDING COLLEAGUES
================================================================================

5.1 Using the Global Search Engine

    The Search Center at http://localhost:5173/search offers three complementary
    ways to explore the community:

    1. Keyword Search:
       Type any clinical condition (e.g., "dot quy", "dong kinh", "Parkinson")
       or technical phrase (e.g., "fMRI", "giai ma tin hieu", "P300"). The engine
       searches across author names, post body text, and specialist profiles.

    2. Category Filter Tabs:
       Filter results into "Tat ca" (All), "Chuyen gia Y te & Nghien cuu"
       (Professionals only), or "Bai viet khoa hoc" (Articles only).

    3. Clinical Hashtags:
       Clicking any hashtag in a post or on the left sidebar immediately scopes
       your search to that precise topic.


5.2 The Specialist Directory

    To locate peers across hospitals or universities, visit the Specialist
    Directory at http://localhost:5173/search?category=professionals.

    This directory groups verified doctors and researchers, highlighting their
    department, hospital or institute, and credential status. It allows an
    attending physician in a provincial hospital to quickly find a stroke
    intervention specialist at a central university hospital, or enables an AI
    engineer to reach out to an electrophysiology lab for collaboration.

CHAPTER 6: CREATING CLINICAL POSTS AND SCHOLARLY INTERACTIONS
================================================================================

6.1 Publishing a Clinical or Research Post

    At the top of the main feed, you will find the post composer card. Creating
    a high-quality post involves:

    1. Composing the Text:
       Write your clinical case summary, research update, or discussion prompt.
       You are encouraged to provide context, cite peer-reviewed literature,
       and include relevant hashtags to help interested colleagues find it.

    2. Attaching Diagnostic Imaging:
       Click the image attachment button to upload up to four clinical images
       (such as MRI slices, CT scans, EEG spectral plots, or flowchart diagrams).
       Each image must be in a supported format (JPEG, PNG, WebP) and cannot
       exceed 5 megabytes.

    3. Publishing:
       Click "Dang bai" (Publish). The post appears at the top of the community
       feed immediately.


6.2 Mandatory Patient Privacy and De-Identification Rule

    Following the core ethical principles of healthcare networks like
    PatientsLikeMe, patient privacy is non-negotiable:

    Before uploading any clinical photograph, MRI, CT, or EEG recording, you
    MUST crop, blur, or completely remove any identifiable patient information,
    including patient full names, medical record numbers (MRN), dates of birth,
    admission timestamps, and identifiable facial features. Failure to de-
    identify patient data violates platform regulations and will lead to
    immediate content removal and potential account suspension.


6.3 Engaging with Scholarly Content

    - Liking Posts:
      Click the heart icon on any post. The platform enforces a database-level
      unique constraint so likes cannot be artificially duplicated.

    - Academic Comments:
      Click the comment button on a post card to expand the discussion thread.
      Use this area to share alternative differential diagnoses, request
      methodology clarifications, or link relevant clinical trials. Authors can
      edit or delete their own comments at any time.

    - Following Peers:
      Clicking "Follow" on a colleague's profile or post card adds their future
      articles to your dedicated "Dang theo doi" (Following) feed tab, allowing
      you to curate your own specialized medical feed.

CHAPTER 7: DIRECT MESSAGING, PEER CONSULTATIONS, AND NOTIFICATIONS
================================================================================

7.1 Private One-on-One Consultations

    While open feed discussions benefit the broader community, certain clinical
    consultations, research grant proposals, or sensitive inquiries require
    privacy. NeuroLink provides a dedicated messaging center at
    http://localhost:5173/messages.

    To start a conversation:
    1. Navigate to the profile of the specialist you wish to consult.
    2. Click the "Nhan tin" (Send Message) button.
    3. The messaging screen opens with that conversation selected. Type your
       message in the input box at the bottom and press Enter or click the send
       arrow.

    Security Guarantee:
    Conversations on NeuroLink are strictly isolated. The backend server verifies
    membership on every message fetch and send attempt. A third party attempting
    to inspect or send messages into a conversation they do not belong to is
    immediately rejected with an HTTP 403 Forbidden status code.


7.2 The Multi-Channel Notification Hub

    The notification center at http://localhost:5173/notifications consolidates
    all events requiring your attention:
    - Post Likes and Comments: Notifies you when a colleague engages with your
      work.
    - New Followers: Informs you when a peer begins following your timeline.
    - Direct Messages: Alerts you to incoming private consultations.
    - Review Decisions: Informs you the moment a reviewer scores your contribution
      or requests revisions.
    - Reward Redemptions: Delivers fulfillment codes or workshop links when your
      credit redemption is approved.

    A convenient "Danh dau da doc tat ca" (Mark All as Read) button at the top
    lets you clear your unread badge counters in one step.

CHAPTER 8: PROFESSIONAL VERIFICATION FOR CLINICIANS AND SCIENTISTS
================================================================================

8.1 Purpose and Eligibility

    Professional verification is the cornerstone of trust on NeuroLink. Ordinary
    members of the general public participate without verification. Verification
    is reserved exclusively for qualified professionals in three distinct tracks:

    Track A: Physicians and Healthcare Providers
      Eligible: Medical doctors, clinical specialists, resident physicians, and
      registered healthcare practitioners holding a valid practicing license
      (Chung chi hanh nghe - CCHN) issued by the Ministry of Health or Provincial
      Department of Health.

    Track B: Neuroscience Researchers and Graduate Fellows
      Eligible: Academic researchers, postdoctoral fellows, and graduate
      research students affiliated with universities or specialized neuroscience
      institutes, holding a verifiable ORCID iD or university research credential.

    Track C: Scientific Workers and Biomedical Engineers
      Eligible: Hospital clinical engineers, neural decoding specialists, and
      biomedical device developers holding institutional staff credentials.


8.2 Submitting Your Credentials Step by Step

    1. Open Settings (http://localhost:5173/settings) and locate the "Xac minh
       danh tinh chuyen mon" (Professional Verification) section.
    2. Select your track (Physician or Researcher).
    3. Enter your professional title, affiliated hospital or institute, and your
       official license number or ORCID identifier.
    4. Attach your supporting document in PDF or high-resolution image format
       (e.g., photo of your practicing license, staff badge, or doctoral diploma).
    5. Click "Gui ho so xac minh" (Submit Verification Request).


8.3 How the Platform Vets Documents

    Once submitted, your request enters the administrative verification queue:
    - An automated Optical Character Recognition (OCR) pipeline scans the
      document, extracting your name, license number, and issuing authority.
    - A cryptographic SHA-256 checksum is recorded, locking the file so any
      subsequent modification or tampering is immediately detectable.
    - An authorized administrator reviews the extracted text against official
      medical registries, confirms your identity, and grants your specialist
      badge.
    - Once approved, your profile receives the verified badge, and you gain full
      access to the Contribution & Rewards module.

CHAPTER 9: HEALTHCARE AND SCIENTIFIC COMMUNITY GUIDELINES
================================================================================

9.1 The Spirit of Collaborative Inquiry

    NeuroLink thrives when discussions remain respectful, intellectually honest,
    and grounded in clinical evidence. We encourage active debate on treatment
    protocols and experimental methodologies, provided disagreements focus on
    data and scientific rationale rather than personal attacks.


9.2 Educational Discussion Versus Medical Advice

    A critical boundary must be respected at all times:
    NeuroLink is a platform for professional peer exchange and medical education.
    It is NOT an online telemedicine clinic. Clinicians must not establish formal
    doctor-patient relationships, issue binding diagnoses, or prescribe
    controlled medications to users through community posts or direct messages.
    General community members reading case discussions must always consult their
    personal healthcare provider for individual medical care.


9.3 Reporting Violations

    If you encounter content that compromises patient confidentiality, spreads
    dangerous medical misinformation, or violates scientific ethics, click the
    flag icon on the post to submit a moderation report. You can categorize the
    concern (e.g., Misinformation, Harassment, Privacy Violation) and provide a
    brief explanation. The administrative team reviews every report in an
    isolated moderation queue.

CHAPTER 10: PRIVACY, SECURITY, AND RESPONSIBLE DATA HANDLING
================================================================================

10.1 Storage and Isolation of Sensitive Files

    Credential files uploaded during professional verification are never stored
    in public website folders. They reside in a secured, private server partition
    accessible exclusively through an authenticated admin API endpoint that logs
    every file inspection to an immutable audit trail.


10.2 Session Lifetime and Token Management

    Your active login session expires automatically after periods of inactivity,
    requiring re-authentication to prevent unauthorized access on shared hospital
    workstations. When logging off from a shared computer, always use the
    explicit "Dang xuat" (Log Out) option in your avatar menu to clear your
    session cookies immediately.


10.3 Research Data Governance

    When discussing clinical trials or patient-derived neuroimaging datasets,
    researchers must ensure their studies complied with their local Institutional
    Review Board (IRB) or Ethics Committee requirements and that proper patient
    consent was secured before sharing aggregate findings.

CHAPTER 11: THE KNOWLEDGE CONTRIBUTION AND PEER REVIEW WORKFLOW
================================================================================

11.1 Purpose of the Contribution Module

    Healthcare specialists and researchers spend hours synthesizing clinical
    findings and educational guides. NeuroLink rewards this dedication through an
    official Contribution & Rewards program.

    Crucially, you DO NOT need to write duplicate articles. You can select any
    existing, high-quality post you have already published on your timeline and
    submit it for reward evaluation.


11.2 Five Eligible Contribution Categories

    When submitting a post, you assign it to one of five academic categories:

    1. HEALTHCARE_EDUCATION
       Educational explanations of neurological diseases, treatment guidelines,
       stroke rehabilitation protocols, and public health awareness.

    2. RESEARCH_CONTRIBUTION
       Summaries of clinical trials, preclinical BCI experiments, neural decoding
       algorithms, and peer-reviewed publication syntheses.

    3. EXPERT_QA
       Detailed, authoritative answers to complex clinical questions posed by
       colleagues or community members.

    4. RESEARCH_DISCUSSION
       In-depth critical appraisals and constructive critiques of published
       methodologies or surgical intervention techniques.

    5. EDUCATIONAL_RESOURCE
       High-value medical infographics, anatomical diagrams, annotated EEG
       reading guides, or procedural video demonstrations.


11.3 Submitting a Post Step by Step

    1. Navigate to the Contribution Center at http://localhost:5173/contributions.
    2. If your account is verified, click "Nop bai dong gop moi" (Submit New).
    3. Choose one of your existing posts from the dropdown selector.
    4. Choose the appropriate contribution category.
    5. Write a concise rationale explaining the post's scientific or educational
       value.
    6. Click "Xac nhan nop ho so" (Confirm Submission).


11.4 The Five Evaluation Criteria

    Appointed clinical reviewers evaluate submissions across five criteria on a
    scale of 1 to 5 stars:
    - Scientific Accuracy (ACCURACY): Reliability of medical claims and evidence.
    - Relevance (RELEVANCE): Direct value to neurology, healthcare, and BCI.
    - Practical Usefulness (USEFULNESS): Actionable utility for clinicians or
      learners.
    - Originality (ORIGINALITY): Novel insight, original synthesis, or firsthand
      clinical observation.
    - Community Impact (COMMUNITY_IMPACT): Fostering constructive engagement and
      scholarly discourse.


11.5 State Machine Transitions and the Anti-Self-Review Rule

    Submissions progress through a strict state machine:
    - DRAFT -> SUBMITTED -> UNDER_REVIEW -> APPROVED (credits awarded)
    - Or: NEEDS_REVISION (author may revise and resubmit)
    - Or: REJECTED (with reviewer feedback)
    - Authors can withdraw a pending submission at any time (WITHDRAWN).

    The Anti-Self-Review Guard:
    A contributor can never review, score, or approve their own submission. The
    backend actively blocks self-review attempts with an HTTP 403 Forbidden
    response, ensuring total independence and credibility.

CHAPTER 12: CREDIT WALLET, IMMUTABLE LEDGER, AND PROFESSIONAL BENEFITS
================================================================================

12.1 The Immutable Append-Only Ledger

    NeuroLink manages credits using dual-entry financial accounting principles:
    - The Transaction Ledger (CreditTransaction table) is the authoritative,
      single source of truth. Every credit awarded, spent, refunded, or adjusted
      is permanently recorded with a timestamp, transaction type, reference ID,
      and idempotency key.
    - The Wallet Balance (CreditWallet) is a cached derived balance. The wallet
      balance is NEVER updated without a corresponding atomic ledger transaction
      inside a database transaction (prisma.$transaction).
    - Historical ledger entries are never edited or deleted.


12.2 Ledger Transaction Types

    - CONTRIBUTION_REWARD (+): Credits awarded for an approved contribution.
    - REWARD_REDEMPTION (-): Credits deducted when requesting a catalog benefit.
    - REFUND (+): Credits automatically refunded if a redemption is cancelled or
      rejected.
    - ADMIN_ADJUSTMENT (+/-): Manual adjustments performed by administrators
      with a mandatory audit justification note.


12.3 Professional Benefits Catalog

    Credits can be redeemed at http://localhost:5173/rewards for career-enhancing
    benefits configured by the administration:
    - Certified CME Online Courses and Training Programs.
    - Full-Access Passes to National and International Neuroscience Conferences.
    - Subscriptions to Academic Research Databases and Medical Libraries.
    - One-on-One Mentoring and Grant Review Sessions with Senior Faculty.
    - Professional Society Event Registrations.


12.4 Strict Anti-Cash-Drain Monetary Guard

    To preserve the educational, non-profit character of the platform and
    prevent financial fraud, monetary cash payouts are DISABLED BY DEFAULT
    (payoutEnabled = false).
    - The platform strictly rejects any payout attempt unless an approved,
      legally vetted platform reward policy is formally enacted by leadership.
    - NeuroLink does NOT support cryptocurrency, blockchain tokens, or peer-to-
      peer credit transfers between users.
    - Credits represent professional recognition within the NeuroLink academic
      ecosystem.


12.5 Redemption Process and Automatic Refunds

    1. Browse available benefits on the Rewards page and click "Doi thuong ngay".
    2. The system checks your available balance, deducts the required credits,
       records a ledger transaction, and creates a redemption request in the
       REQUESTED state.
    3. An administrator reviews the request and fulfills it (e.g., providing a
       CME enrollment voucher code or conference registration confirmation).
    4. If the item is out of stock, or if you cancel your pending request, the
       system issues an automated REFUND transaction, returning 100% of your
       credits to your wallet instantly.

CHAPTER 13: ADMINISTRATOR GOVERNANCE, REVIEW QUEUES, AND SYSTEM MODERATION
================================================================================

13.1 Overview of the Admin Dashboard

    Authorized administrators access the unified control center at
    http://localhost:5173/admin. The dashboard is organized into five operational
    tabs:

    Tab 1: Tham dinh Bac si/ORCID (Credential Verification Queue)
      Displays pending verification dossiers. Admins inspect the applicant's
      hospital affiliation, review automated OCR extractions, check file SHA-256
      hashes, and approve or reject the credential claim.

    Tab 2: Xet duyet Dong gop (Contribution Review Queue)
      Reviewers claim pending contributions, review the linked post, score the
      five evaluation criteria from 1 to 5 stars, record constructive review
      notes, set the credit award amount, and submit the decision (Approve,
      Reject, or Request Revision).

    Tab 3: Phan thuong & Doi qua (Rewards Catalog & Redemption Fulfillment)
      Admins manage the reward catalog (adding new CME courses, adjusting credit
      costs, toggling active status) and fulfill pending member redemption
      requests by issuing access codes or workshop tickets.

    Tab 4: Quan ly Vi Tin chi (Wallet Audit and Adjustments)
      Provides an administrative overview of total credits in circulation. Admins
      can search for any member wallet, view full ledger transaction histories,
      and perform manual adjustments with mandatory audit reasons.

    Tab 5: Bao cao Vi pham (Moderation Reports Queue)
      Surfaces community-flagged posts and comments. Admins review reported
      violations, resolve or dismiss reports, and hide or remove offending
      content.

CHAPTER 14: TROUBLESHOOTING, FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS, AND GLOSSARY
================================================================================

14.1 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

    Q: Can ordinary users without a medical degree join NeuroLink?
    A: Yes. General users can register, read public posts, follow doctors, and
       participate in general discussions. Only clinical and research badges
       require professional verification.

    Q: Why was my post submission for rewards rejected?
    A: Submissions are evaluated against scientific accuracy, clinical relevance,
       usefulness, originality, and impact. If a post lacks scientific evidence
       or does not fit the selected contribution category, reviewers provide
       feedback in the review history. You can address the feedback and resubmit
       if revisions were requested.

    Q: Can I transfer my earned credits to a colleague's account?
    A: No. Peer-to-peer credit transfers are prohibited to prevent secondary
       markets and maintain the integrity of professional recognition.

    Q: What happens if an approved post is later discovered to be plagiarized?
    A: Administrators can revoke the contribution's approved status and execute
       a ledger reversal adjustment, deducting the awarded credits.


14.2 Troubleshooting Common Issues

    - Unable to upload image in post composer:
      Check that your file is in JPEG, PNG, or WebP format and that its size is
      under 5 MB. Large high-resolution scan files should be exported at a
      standard web resolution before uploading.

    - "Thao tac bi tu choi (403)" when reviewing a contribution:
      You are likely attempting to review a contribution you authored. The Anti-
      Self-Review policy prevents authors from scoring their own submissions.
      Have another administrator evaluate the post.

    - "Rut tien mat hien chua duoc kich hoat" error:
      Monetary payouts are disabled by default under our Anti-Cash-Drain
      monetary guard. Redeem your credits for professional benefits such as CME
      courses or conference passes instead.


14.3 Platform and Healthcare Glossary

    - BCI (Brain-Computer Interface):
      Direct communication pathways between an enhanced or wired brain and an
      external computational device.

    - CCHN (Chung chi hanh nghe):
      Official healthcare practicing license issued by the Ministry of Health or
      authorized state medical boards.

    - CME (Continuing Medical Education):
      Educational activities designed to maintain, develop, or increase the
      knowledge, skills, and professional performance of healthcare providers.

    - De-identification:
      The process of removing personal identifiers from clinical records and
      imaging studies to protect patient confidentiality.

    - EEG (Electroencephalography):
      An electrophysiological monitoring method used to record electrical
      activity of the brain.

    - fMRI (Functional Magnetic Resonance Imaging):
      Neuroimaging technique measuring brain activity by detecting changes
      associated with blood flow.

    - Immutable Ledger:
      An append-only database record of transactions that cannot be edited or
      deleted once written, guaranteeing financial auditability.

    - ORCID (Open Researcher and Contributor ID):
      A persistent digital identifier that distinguishes individual researchers
      and links them to their scientific contributions.
